Philippine map validation data, version 1 (2026-11-03).

Land: geoBoundaries gbOpen PHL ADM0, revision 9469f09,
boundary ID PHL-ADM0-24100683. Source: OCHA Philippines and NAMRIA.

Original: https://media.githubusercontent.com/media/wmgeolab/geoBoundaries/9469f09/releaseData/gbOpen/PHL/ADM0/geoBoundaries-PHL-ADM0.geojson

Inland water: GSHHG 2.3.7 full resolution. Paul Wessel (SOEST,
University of Hawai'i) and Walter H. F. Smith (NOAA).
License: GNU LGPL v3 or later; see GSHHG-LICENSE.TXT,
GSHHG-COPYINGv3, and GSHHG-COPYING.LESSERv3 in this directory.
Original: https://www.soest.hawaii.edu/pwessel/gshhg/gshhg-bin-2.3.7.zip

Modified: geographic subset, 0.00010-degree simplification, six-decimal
delta encoding with per-polygon bounds. Approximate map guard, not a
legal boundary survey; not every small waterway or recent reclamation
is represented. No distance buffer is applied to accept offshore pins.

Reproduction script: scripts/build_philippine_map_data.py in the
application source repository. No new runtime package is required.
