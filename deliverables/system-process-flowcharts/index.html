How to read these charts

  • Matching is decision support, not the provider's final selection decision.
  • Exam and interview stages appear only when configured for a program.
  • Online documents support initial checking; providers may require originals for complete verification.
  • The application deadline closes new applications, while already submitted records continue through review.
  • Monitoring begins after selection and acceptance of the recipient agreement.
