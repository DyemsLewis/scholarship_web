155 [Heading2] Define qualifications and the scoring plan
156 [] Signed in account: nuvareya.programs
157 [] Program configuration determines admission safeguards and later assessment. Use a draft when demonstrating a change; do not change an intake that already has fixed evaluation snapshots.
158 [] 1. In Eligibility, choose the intended education level and qualification, grade scale, year level, location and other rules. Remove irrelevant inherited restrictions rather than relying on free-text descriptions.
159 [] 2. Separate mandatory rules, reviewable qualifications and preferences according to the available controls. A preference contributes guidance; it is not the same as a mandatory exclusion.
160 [] 3. In the selection setup, choose the actual screening, formal verification, exam, interview and final decision stages needed by the program. Explain their order to applicants.
161 [] 4. Inspect the preset weights for the scored stages. Submission freezes the scoring plan for that intake; formal application is a qualifying check, not extra numeric points.
162 [] 5. Review support terms, deadline order, recipient slots and application intake limit before submitting the draft. Publication review remains an admin decision.
163 [] Expected result and handoff: The published program supplies clear rules and a stable evaluation basis. The admin reviewer checks publication readiness separately from applicant selection.
